Empire – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What Empire is

Empire is a free browser strategy game. This page explains what information the game handles.

Gameplay clips

While you play, the game may record a short clip (about 10 seconds) of the game screen, together with your empire name and difficulty level. The clip contains only the game view, not your camera, microphone, screen outside the game, or personal files. Clips may be posted publicly on the Empire YouTube channel to promote the game.

Analytics

The game uses PostHog analytics to count game starts, results and similar usage events so we can improve the game. We do not ask for your name, email address or any account details to play.

YouTube

The Empire server uses the YouTube Data API only to upload clips to the Empire channel owned by the developer. It does not access the YouTube accounts or data of players. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and removal

Clips waiting to be uploaded are stored temporarily on the server and deleted after upload. To ask for a clip to be removed from YouTube, contact us at the email below.
